About 个人简介

I am a Ph.D. student working on integrated circuits at Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT). My research focuses on infrared target detection and brain-inspired (neuromorphic) computing, with interests in deep learning for infrared small target detection, spiking neural networks, and their hardware implementation.

本人现为北京邮电大学集成电路方向在读博士研究生，研究兴趣集中在 红外目标检测与类脑研究，包括红外弱小目标检测的深度学习方法、 脉冲神经网络及其硬件实现。

Research Interests 研究方向

Infrared Target Detection Infrared Small Target Detection Brain-inspired Computing Neuromorphic Computing Spiking Neural Networks Deep Learning
